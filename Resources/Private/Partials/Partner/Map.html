The partner map: its assets, the map element and the partners it draws. Arguments: - partners: the partners to draw, as the map plugin passes its result. - partner: a single partner instead, as a page template passes the partner of its page. The map is left out unless the partner has coordinates. - map: the map settings, "settings.map" of the plugin and "mapSettings" of the partner page. Without them the map module uses its defaults. Several maps on one page are not supported: the element ids are fixed.
