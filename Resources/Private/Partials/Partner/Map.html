The partner map: its assets, the map element and the partners it draws. Arguments: - partners: the partners to draw, as the map plugin passes its result. - partner: a single partner instead, as a page template passes the partner of its page. The map is left out unless the partner has coordinates and "Show on map" switched on. - map: the map settings, "settings.map" of the plugin and "mapSettings" of the partner page. Without them the map module uses its defaults. - assets: the switch of the map script, "settings.assets" of the plugin and "mapAssets" of the partner page. "assets.js" switched off leaves out the map module and the stylesheets of the map libraries it imports, and the map element stays empty until a script of the site draws it. Without the argument the partial loads them, as it did before the switch existed. Several maps on one page are not supported: the element ids are fixed. The map libraries are ES modules, imported by map.js through the import map. Only their stylesheets are registered here, from the same version directories: the map does not work without them. Sizing the map element is up to the site, Leaflet draws nothing into an element without a height. They belong to the script: a site that brings its own leaves out both. A caller that passes no "assets" gets them, see the arguments above.
