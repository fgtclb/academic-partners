The navigation between the pages of a paginated partner list. Every link carries the demand of the list shown - filter and sorting - and the page it leads to. The section "PageLink" renders one; its text is the page number, or the label "labelKey" names for first, previous, next and last. "hasLessPages" and "hasMorePages" exist on the numbered pagination only: the core pagination links every page and renders no ellipsis. Arguments: - paginator: the paginator, for the current page - pagination: the pagination, for the pages to link - demandArguments: the demand of the list shown, as a URL carries it
